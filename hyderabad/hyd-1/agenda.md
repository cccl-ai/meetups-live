## About

Hyderabad's first CCCL event — Claude Code Central London comes to Hyderabad. Eight community speakers from engineering, product, HR and founding across Knacklabs, Welspun GCC, Aganitha, Razor Group, Futurely, TryNewAI, Kinara AI and Enaiblr AI — sharing how they build with Claude every day. Co-hosted with Knacklabs. Organised by Vikram Pawar (Anthropic Ambassador).
